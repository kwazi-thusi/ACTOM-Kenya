// Why Us

Your Partner in Energy, Your Ally in Progress

For over a century, ACTOM has stood as a beacon of reliability and expertise in the electro-mechanical industry. Our rich history is woven with innovation, allowing us to evolve continually to meet the changing energy needs of Africa. From comprehensive manufacturing and distribution to top-tier repair and maintenance services, we offer a full range of solutions that make us more than just a provider — we are a partner in energy. With a vast network of 33 strategically placed outlets across Sub-Saharan Africa, we ensure our clients receive prompt, localized support wherever they are.

What sets ACTOM apart is not just our experience, but our commitment to sustainability and cutting-edge technology. We pride ourselves on delivering environmentally conscious solutions that empower industries and communities while minimizing ecological impact. Trusted by industry leaders, ACTOM’s dedication to quality, efficiency, and forward-thinking practices positions us as the go-to partner for reliable and future-focused energy solutions.
